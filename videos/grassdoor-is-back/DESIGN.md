# Grassdoor — design truth for this film

Concept: **the door is open again.** Grassdoor's mark is a door; the film opens a door onto the
reopening and closes it again on "your next order is waiting" — the loop is the next knock.

## Palette (from Figma "Text & Colors")
- Teal primary `#05A0A8` · dark `#056D76` · darker `#063A3F` · light `#60C2C7`
- Near-black stage `#030909` / `#020808`, white `#FFFFFF`
- Sticker teal `#028E99`, sticker dark `#0B4F57`, star gold `#F9C74F`

## Type
- Display: **Anton** (brand banners "We are back") — all caps, tight leading 0.9
- Script accent: **Meow Script** ("we've missed you")
- UI / body: **Inter** 500–900
- Sticker lettering: **Titan One** (closest match to the printed sticker), sub-lines Montserrat 800

## Motion language
Deliberate, weighted, never bouncy-cartoon: `power3.out` entrances, `power3.inOut` camera moves,
`expo.out` mask reveals. Lines reveal from masks bottom-up, one beat apart (0.12s). Every text line
holds at least 1.0s fully legible. Scenes hand off with overlapping crossfade + depth push
(outgoing drifts up/blurs, incoming settles from 1.08 scale). Backgrounds always drift slowly.
