You direct Atlas, the orchestrator. Atlas runs five team leads, each with its own sub-agents, plus Penny on budgets, a Brand Vault everyone reads, and Vera at the QA gate. Every agent has a token budget and pings you when it is missing a skill, a file, access or a decision. Click any node or row.
